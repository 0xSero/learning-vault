# Study Plan

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> Four weeks through the 604 self-referencing sayings, in canonical order, ~20 a day, each one opened in full context. Week 3 is John (44% of the corpus) and the week you "blow your mind" on the "I am" declarations. The plan is built so that by the end you have read the whole of Jesus speaking about himself — and you know which sayings he uses for himself, in which order, and what the data says about where he talks about himself most.

## The daily ritual (same every day)

1. **Read ~20 sayings** from the day's block, in canonical order (the [[Self Reference Catalog]] is grouped exactly this way).
2. **Open each reference in context** — the catalog links each saying to its verse at Bible Gateway (KJV). Read the surrounding 3–5 verses. The sayings are sentences; the context is the argument.
3. **Mark the "I am" sayings** (the catalog flags them with ◈). Do not analyze them yet; just collect them.
4. **One journal line per day** — one sentence, your own words: what surprised you. No theology, no defense — just the observation.

## Week 1 — Matthew (122 sayings)

**Theme:** the King who comes as a man.
- Days 1–3: Matt 4–11 (the call, the temptation, "come unto me," "I am meek and lowly in heart," "all things are delivered unto me of my Father").
- Days 4–6: Matt 12–16 (the son-of-man sayings, "whom say ye that I am?", "upon this rock I will build my church").
- Days 7–8: Matt 17–23 (the transfiguration, "I will build my church" continued, the temple, "ye shall not see me henceforth").
- Days 9–10: Matt 24–28 (the Olivet discourse — "my words shall not pass away," "I come quickly" — and the resurrection: "all power is given unto me in heaven and in earth").

**Week 1 question to hold:** When Matthew's Jesus speaks about himself, is he talking about *what he is* or *what he is doing*? (The data's answer: both, but the doing is always in service of the identity.)

## Week 2 — Mark (60) + Luke (108)

**Theme:** the servant and the traveler.
- Days 1–3: Mark, all of it (the shortest gospel, the tightest self-reference: "the Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister").
- Days 4–7: Luke 2–10 (the child in the temple, "the Spirit of the Lord is upon me," "who touched me," "he that heareth you heareth me").
- Days 8–10: Luke 11–24 (the bread and the cross: "with desire I have desired to eat this passover with you before I suffer," the passion week, "behold my hands and my feet, that it is I myself").

**Week 2 question to hold:** Mark and Luke never use "I am the X" — where does that push? (Answer: it all accumulates in John. Go there in week 3.)

## Week 3 — John (263 sayings) — the core

**Theme:** the Word who says "I am."
Read chapter by chapter, in this order — it is the thread's phase 5 in full:

| Day | Chapters | What you're reading |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | John 1–2 (self-refs) + 4 | The Word; the well ("I that speak unto thee am he"); "I have meat to eat" |
| 2 | John 5 | The authority chapter: "the Son can do nothing of himself…", "I receive not honour from men" |
| 3 | John 6 | **The bread discourse** — the whole [[The Sacrifice Thread]] in first person |
| 4 | John 7–8 | The temple dispute; "Before Abraham was, I am" (see [[John 8:40]]) |
| 5 | John 9–10 | The light; the shepherd: "no man taketh it from me, but I lay it down of myself" |
| 6 | John 11–12 | "I am the resurrection, and the life"; "for this cause came I unto this hour" |
| 7 | John 13 | The washing of feet; "a new commandment I give unto you" |
| 8 | John 14 | "I am the way, the truth, and the life"; "I and the Father" |
| 9 | John 15–16 | The vine; "ye have not chosen me, but I have chosen you" |
| 10 | John 17 | **The High Priestly Prayer** — the whole thread in a prayer |
| 11 | John 18–21 | "I am he" (the garden); "my kingdom is not of this world"; the resurrection; "follow me" |

**Week 3 question to hold:** Count the ◈ sayings as you go. By the end of the week you'll have met all 82. Which one is the one you'd keep? (There is no wrong answer; the data can't rank them, only you can.)

## Week 4 — Acts + Epistles + Revelation (51) + the reread

**Theme:** the risen voice and the return.
- Days 1–2: Acts 1, 9, 22, 26 (the road to Damascus, three times; "I am Jesus whom thou persecutest").
- Days 3: 1 Cor 11 + 2 Cor 12 (the table; "my grace is sufficient for thee").
- Days 4: Revelation 1–3 + 22 (the risen Christ's letters; "I am Alpha and Omega"; "I come quickly").
- Days 5–7: **The reread.** Open the "I am declarations" filter on the live site (or the ◈-flagged sayings in the catalog) and read all 82 in one sitting, in order. This is the payoff: the whole identity-claim of the Gospels as a single sequence.

## The tools

- **The live site:** https://0xsero.github.io/bible-quotes-wiki/self-references.html — filters (All / "I am" declarations / search / book) do the daily chunking for you.
- **The catalog:** [[Self Reference Catalog]] — the full 604, offline, with the ◈ flags.
- **The threads:** [[Devotional Threads]] — if you'd rather read by theme than by book, start there instead of week 1.

> [!tip] The rule of the plan
> Read the context before you judge the saying. A saying read alone is a slogan; the same saying with its three surrounding verses is an argument. The plan is 20 sayings a day, not 20 arguments a day — the arguments come from the context you open.
