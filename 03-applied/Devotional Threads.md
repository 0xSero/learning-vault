# Devotional Threads

> [!abstract] TL;DR
> Five reading threads through the 604 self-referencing sayings, each a curated subset with a theme and an arc. Use these when you want to read by *theme* instead of by *book* (the [[Study Plan]] is the book order; this is the thematic order). Each thread is short enough to read in a day or two and long enough to actually change how you read the rest.

## Thread 1 — The Bread Thread

**Theme:** God's life is self-giving; creation lives by that giving.
The [[The Sacrifice Thread]] compressed to its first-person form.

Read in order:
1. John 6:32 — "Moses gave you not that bread from heaven; but my Father giveth you the true bread from heaven."
2. John 6:35 — "I am the bread of life."
3. John 6:48–51 — "I am that bread of life… the bread that I will give is my flesh, which I will give for the life of the world."
4. John 6:53–57 — "Except ye eat the flesh of the Son of man… he that eateth me shall live by me."
5. John 6:63 — "The words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life."
6. John 4:32–34 — "I have meat to eat that ye know not of… my meat is to do the will of him that sent me."
7. Luke 22:19–20 — "This is my body which is given for you."
8. 1 Cor 11:24–25 — "This is my body, which is broken for you: this do in remembrance of me."

**The thread's single question:** Is the giving *for* the world, or *into* God? (The data's answer, every time: *for* — "for the life of the world," "for you," "for many." Nothing flows back in.)

## Thread 2 — The I Am Thread (82 sayings)

**Theme:** the identity claims, in sequence.
All 82 ◈-flagged sayings in the [[Self Reference Catalog]], in canonical order. The seven high claims (bread, light, door, shepherd, resurrection-and-life, way-truth-life, vine) are the backbone; the rest are the supporting assertions ("Before Abraham was, I am"; "I and my Father are one"; "I am he").
**How to read it:** one sitting, in order, no notes. Then reread just the seven high claims. The gap between the two readings is what the thread is for.

## Thread 3 — The Son of Man Thread (~69 sayings)

**Theme:** the title that pulls in two directions — fully man, and the one given the kingdom.
All the "the Son of man" sayings in the catalog (search the catalog for "Son of man"). Read in the three clusters of [[Son of Man]]:
1. **Humble:** Matt 8:20; Mark 10:45; Luke 9:58 (no place to lay his head; came to minister).
2. **Authoritative:** Mark 2:10; Mark 2:28; Luke 19:10 (forgive sins; Lord of the sabbath; seek and save).
3. **Exalted:** Mark 13:26; Matt 24:30; Matt 26:64; John 3:13; John 5:27 (the clouds; the right hand; in heaven; judgment).
**The thread's single question:** Can a title mean two opposite things at once? (The data's answer: Jesus held both levels, and the range *is* the meaning.)

## Thread 4 — The Father Thread

**Theme:** "I and my Father are one" — the relationship as self-definition.
1. John 5:19 — "The Son can do nothing of himself, but what he seeth the Father do."
2. John 5:30 — "I seek not mine own will, but the will of the Father."
3. John 8:29 — "He that sent me is with me: the Father hath not left me alone."
4. John 10:15–18 — "As the Father knoweth me… no man taketh it from me, but I lay it down of myself."
5. John 10:30 — "I and my Father are one."
6. John 14:9–11 — "He that hath seen me hath seen the Father."
7. John 16:15 — "All things that the Father hath are mine."
8. **John 17, the whole chapter** — the High Priestly Prayer, read slowly, twice.
**The thread's single question:** What is the difference between "my Father" (relationship) and "the Father in me, and I in him" (oneness)? (The data's answer: the Gospels use both, and the difference is the distance the cross closes.)

## Thread 5 — The Cross-Week Thread

**Theme:** the one week where the self-reference becomes the self-giving.
All the passion-week sayings in the catalog:
- **Matthew 26** (18 sayings) — "one of you shall betray me"; "this is my body"; "my soul is exceeding sorrowful"; "after I am risen again, I will go before you into Galilee."
- **Mark 14** (20) — "it is one of the twelve, that dippeth with me in the dish"; "this is my blood of the new testament."
- **Luke 22** (24) — "with desire I have desired to eat this passover with you before I suffer"; "I am among you as he that serveth"; "the things concerning me have an end."
- **John 18** (9) — "the cup which my Father hath given me, shall I not drink it?"; "my kingdom is not of this world"; "to this end was I born."
**How to read it:** read the four accounts *together*, chapter by chapter, not one after another. The four voices saying the same week about themselves is where the corpus stops being data and becomes a room.

> [!tip] How threads and the study plan relate
> The [[Study Plan]] is the survey (all 604, in order). The threads are the close readings (subsets, by theme). Do the survey first, then return to a thread — or start with a thread if a theme is calling, and let the survey be the catch-up.
